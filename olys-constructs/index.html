PRIVATE RESIDENCE

The Arden Residence

LOCATION Islamabad, Pakistan
YEAR 2026
AREA 14,500 Sq. Ft.

Designed as an elevated sanctuary in harmony with the surrounding natural landscape, The Arden Residence integrates monolithic fair-faced concrete, floor-to-ceiling high-efficiency glazing, and expansive shaded cantilevers.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

  • Structural System: Reinforced concrete core with post-tensioned floor cantilevers
  • Envelope: Low-E acoustic double-glazed curtain walls & teak rainscreens
  • Passive Design: Cross-ventilation breezeways & thermal mass orientation
  • Handover: Fully turnkey commissioned with custom millwork
